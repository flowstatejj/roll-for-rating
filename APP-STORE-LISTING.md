## Description (max 4000)
```
Rollr brings a real, earned rating to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu — like a chess rating for the mats.

Most rolls are forgotten the moment they end. Rollr makes them count. Agree to a match with a training partner in the app, have a third person referee and record the result, and your Elo rating moves with every win, loss, and draw. No more guessing who's leveling up — let the mats decide.

HOW IT WORKS
• Challenge a partner in the app and set up where and when to meet (an open mat, after class, wherever).
• A referee or witness records the result — submission, points, or draw.
• Your rating updates instantly and your record is saved.

BUILD YOUR RATING
• A true Elo rating that rises and falls with real results.
• Win streaks, stats, and a full match history.
• Optional rating wagers — stake points on a match and let the winner take them (adults only).

CLIMB THE LADDER
• Global and seasonal leaderboards.
• Gym power rankings, teams, and tournaments.
• Rivalries, titles, and achievements to chase.

TRAIN YOUR GAME
• BJJ puzzles with instant feedback to sharpen your knowledge between rolls.
• Daily quests and streaks to keep you coming back.

BUILT FOR THE COMMUNITY
• Find open mats and people to roll with in your gym network.
• Message inside a match to set up the meet.
• Publish standout matches so teammates can watch and react.

SAFE FOR YOUNGER GRAPPLERS
• Accounts for members under 18 require a parent or guardian's approval before they can compete.
• Younger members get extra protections: no wagering, and limited public visibility.

Whether you're a white belt chasing your first stripe or a competitor tracking every roll, Rollr turns training into progress you can see.

Train. Roll. Rank up.
```
