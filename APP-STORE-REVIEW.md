## Description
Roll for Rating turns everyday training into a real, earned rating for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu — like a chess rating for the mats.

• Agree to a roll, have a teammate referee it (or film it and both confirm), and your ROR adjusts based on who you beat.
• Climb global, gym, and local leaderboards.
• Run and join LEAGUES — local clubs that meet weekly with their own standings.
• Build and run TOURNAMENTS — round-robin or brackets, individual or team, multiple mats with referees.
• Train BJJ puzzles, chase submission collections, and earn belt-calibrated achievements.

Built for the whole gym — adults and (with verified parent/guardian consent) younger grapplers, who use non-photographic warrior avatars instead of photos.

The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.

SUBSCRIPTION / PAYWALL
- The app is gated by one auto-renewable subscription: Roll for Rating Pro, $4.99/month with a 7-day free trial.
- On a new account, the paywall appears after sign-up/onboarding. It shows the price, trial terms, the auto-renewal disclosure, Restore Purchases, links to Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, and a sign-out option.
- Purchases are verified server-side with the App Store Server API (sandbox supported); renewals/cancellations sync via App Store Server Notifications V2.
- To test the purchase flow itself, create a fresh account and subscribe with a sandbox Apple ID; access unlocks immediately after purchase.

WHAT TO TEST
- Sign in with the demo account (it skips the paywall).
- Community tab → Find a Roll / Leagues / Tournaments.
- Profile → tap avatar → "Choose a warrior" (avatar builder).
- Tap any other user → Challenge, and the Block and Report buttons (Trust & Safety).
- Profile → Delete account (in-app account deletion; works end-to-end). Deletion is permanent — if possible please test it on a fresh sandbox account rather than the shared demo account, so the demo credentials remain valid.

TRUST & SAFETY (Guideline 1.2)
- Block users (hides them and prevents matches), Report users/content (moderation queue, reviewed within 24h), and a Terms & Community Guidelines agreement with zero tolerance for objectionable content is required at signup.

MINORS / COPPA
- Under-18 requires verified parent/guardian email consent before competing; under-14 accounts are protected (not publicly searchable, matched only with other minors).
- Minors NEVER upload photos — they use non-photographic "warrior" avatars rendered on-device. We do not collect images of minors.
- Minors get access via the same subscription; for a minor's purchase, Apple's Ask to Buy routes the purchase to the parent/guardian.

"WAGER" IS NOT GAMBLING
- "ROR" and any "wager" are virtual in-app points only. They have NO monetary value, cannot be purchased, redeemed, or exchanged for money or anything of value. There is no real-money gambling. The only purchase in the app is the Roll for Rating Pro subscription. (Stated in the Terms.)
