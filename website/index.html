Questions

What is ROR?

ROR is your rating — short for Roll for Rating, and it rhymes with "roar." It's an Elo-style number that goes up when you win and down when you lose, so it reflects real, earned skill rather than self-reported belts or claims.

How are matches verified?

Matches happen in person. Both grapplers agree to the match in the app, and a neutral third person referees and records the result. Both sides confirm, so the rating only moves on real, witnessed rolls.

Is it gi or no-gi?

Both. Roll for Rating works for any Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu or grappling match — gi, no-gi, or submission-only.

Is it free?

Yes — Roll for Rating is free to download and use. We'll always keep the core rating and leaderboards free.

Can kids use it?

Younger grapplers are welcome with protections. Accounts for members under 18 need a parent or guardian's approval before competing, and under-14 accounts are managed by a parent with extra privacy — never public, never searchable.
