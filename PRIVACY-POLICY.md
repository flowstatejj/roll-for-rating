## Information we collect

When you create an account and use the app, we collect:

- **Account info:** email address, username, display name, and belt rank.
- **Date of birth:** used to determine your age tier (adult / teen / under-14)
  and to apply the protections described below. For members under 18 we also
  collect a **parent or guardian's email address** to obtain consent.
- **Profile details you add:** city/area, and whether you are open for a challenge.
- **Activity data:** matches you take part in (opponents, referee, result,
  ratings), puzzle answers and scores, quests, streaks, and similar in-app
  activity used to compute your rating, stats, and leaderboards.
- **Optional media:** photos or match videos you choose to upload, and your
  camera/photo-library access only at the moment you attach them.

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers,
and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking.

## How we use it

- To run the core features: matching, ratings/Elo, leaderboards, puzzles,
  messaging within a match, and notifications.
- To keep minors safe (see below).
- To maintain and secure the service.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Service providers

We share data only with vendors that operate the app on our behalf:

- **Supabase** — database, authentication, storage, and hosting of our backend.
- **Anthropic (Claude)** — when you submit a written puzzle answer, the question
  and your typed answer are sent to Anthropic's API to grade it. (Not used to
  train their models.)
- **Resend** — sends the parental-consent approval email for minor accounts.

## Minors (under 18)

We take extra steps for younger members:

- Accounts for members under 18 are created **restricted** and cannot compete
  until a **parent or guardian approves** the account via an emailed link.
- Minors cannot wager rating, and their profiles are not publicly searchable
  until approved.
- **Under-14** accounts are further limited: no public matches, no leaderboard,
  not searchable, and they can only be matched against other under-18 members.
- A parent/guardian may request access to or deletion of their child's data at
  **[FILL IN CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## Your choices and data deletion

- You can **delete your account at any time** from **Profile → Delete account**.
  This permanently removes your account, profile, matches, and stats.
- You may also email **[FILL IN CONTACT EMAIL]** to request access to or
  deletion of your data.

## Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account,
your associated records are removed from our database. Backups are purged on a
rolling basis.
